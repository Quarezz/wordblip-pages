WordBlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

WordBlip is published by Ruslan Nikolayev. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Information Stored On Device

WordBlip stores reading state, training progress, session history, imported documents, and app settings locally on your device so the app can function properly.

Imported Content

If you import TXT, EPUB, or PDF files, that content is processed and stored locally on your device for reading and training features. Imported documents are not uploaded to a WordBlip server.

Analytics

WordBlip uses Google Analytics 4 (through Firebase) and PostHog for minimal product analytics, such as screen views and onboarding, training, reading session, and paywall events. They are used to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Imported reading content and file names are never sent.

  • PostHog stores this data in the EU.
  • Events are tied to a random ID that the app creates when it is installed. This ID is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID, and WordBlip never asks you to sign in.
  • Your approximate location (such as country and city) is worked out from your IP address. The IP address is then discarded.
  • Events also include basic technical details: app version, device model, operating system version, and language.
  • WordBlip does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), shows no ads, and contains no advertising SDKs. Analytics data is not used for advertising or shared with advertisers.

Purchases And Subscriptions

WordBlip uses RevenueCat and Apple’s App Store infrastructure to manage subscriptions, purchase status, and restore purchases. Purchase-related information may be processed by those providers to confirm entitlement access.

Accounts

WordBlip does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Data Sharing

WordBlip does not sell your personal data. Service providers process data only to run analytics (Google Analytics 4 and PostHog) and subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat).

Your Choices

  • You can remove imported files and local reading data from the app.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can contact support with privacy questions.

Contact
