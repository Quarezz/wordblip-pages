WordBlip Support

If you need help with WordBlip, contact support and include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Contact

Email: morfed.quarezz@gmail.com

What WordBlip Supports

  • RSVP reading with adjustable speed and ORP highlighting
  • Guided training sessions with Easy, Medium, and Hard levels
  • Progress tracking and session history
  • Document import for TXT, EPUB, and PDF files
  • Subscription restore and premium access questions

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app after changing subscription or purchase state.
  • Use Restore Purchases if premium access does not appear immediately.
  • Some scanned PDFs may not import correctly because they do not contain extractable text.
